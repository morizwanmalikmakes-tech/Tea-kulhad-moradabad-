OUR STORY

Ek chhota sa carwaan, ek bada sa idea

The Tea Factory ki shuruaat ek seedhi si soch se hui — Moradabad ko ek aisi jagah chahiye jahan chai sirf chai na ho, ek ehsaas ho. Dindayal Nagar ke is chhote se corner me humne banaya hai ek aisa cafe jahan ambience cute hai, seating aaram ki hai, aur har plate me wahi pyaar hai jo ghar ki rasoi me hota hai.

Log yahan adrak chai ke liye aate hain, sizzling brownie pe ruk jaate hain, aur ambience ke deewane ho ke lautte hain — isi liye Google pe 4.9★ aur 37 se zyada pyaar bhare reviews hamari sabse badi daulat hain.

Aur ab hum badal rahe hain — online ordering, live order tracking aur ghar tak delivery ke saath, taaki jo pyaar cafe me milta hai, wo ab aapke ghar ke sofa tak bhi pahuche. Hamara promise simple hai: acha khana, sahi daam, aur muskurahat free.
